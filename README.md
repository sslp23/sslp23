# Sergio Pessoa 🇧🇷

Hey, I'm Sergio, a computer engineer from Recife, Pernambuco, now working as a Data Scientist
at **Insight Sport**, where I build analytics for football clubs.

### What I'm into

- ⚽ **Football analytics** — this has been my day job for the past 3 years: player valuation,
  event data, and performance models used by professional clubs.
- 📈 **Quantitative analysis** — finance and research: signal building, backtesting, and
  anything that rewards being right about uncertainty.
- 🤖 **Machine Learning & Deep Learning** — mostly applied, mostly to things that move.
- 📊 **Applied statistics** — Bayesian shrinkage, Poisson models, and being suspicious of
  my own metrics.
- 🧠 **AI development** — building with LLMs and agents, and figuring out where they actually
  earn their keep in a data workflow.
